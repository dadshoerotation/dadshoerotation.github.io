The Work Dress Shoe

You need a quality leather lace-up oxford that lasts at least five years, depending on how often you wear it to work. Make it dark brown.

If you already own sneaker-soled brown leather oxfords, they're fine for now, but don't buy them new.

For comfort, get a sneaker-like insole but not a sneaker-like midsole. You can add an aftermarket insole like Move, or buy from Cole Haan or Wolf & Shepherd, but never a sneaker-type midsole.

Insoles are a requirement for dad shoes, dress shoes included. See my insole picks →
