Insoles: the dad shoe requirement

A good aftermarket insole can turn any old shoe into something springy and supportive. For dad shoes, it’s not optional. It’s a requirement.

Dad Shoe Matt

Why every dad shoe needs one

Most shoes, even good ones, ship with a flat, thin insole that does almost nothing. Pull it out and drop in a real one, and the same shoe feels springier and more supportive.

That’s why I treat insoles as a requirement for every pair in the rotation, from the everyday shoe to the work dress shoe. Put a good insole in a shoe you already own before you shop for a new pair.

Nike Zoom is the best springy cushion ever invented, and nothing even comes close.

My opinion, Dad Shoe Matt

If you want the real thing in a shoe, my Classics pick for the Wildcard slot, the Nike Zoom Vomero 5, has Zoom Air built in. For everything else, an insole gets you a lot of the way there.

Pick 2

Zoom-style air insoles

The other move is a cheap pair of aftermarket Zoom-style air insoles, the knockoffs sold on Amazon, AliExpress, Temu and DHgate. They put springy air pods under your foot for not much money.

These are aftermarket insoles with Zoom-style air cushioning. They are not made by Nike and have nothing to do with Nike.

  • Goofort DynaZoom insolesAir cushion in the heel, with arch support. $17.28 a pair on Amazon (size 9–10, 28 cm).
  • Full-length Zoom Air cushion insolesA full-length air cushion insole from a seller in China. US $18.67–$22.24 a pair on DHgate, depending on the option.

Check the sizing chart before you order, and pull out the shoe’s original insole first. Most of these can be trimmed with scissors to fit. Quality on cheap insoles varies from pair to pair.
